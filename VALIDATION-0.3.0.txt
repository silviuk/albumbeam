PhotosBeam 0.3.0 — local camera timeline

Implemented:
- Explicit phone Start / refresh camera feed with Android 8+ media permissions,
  Android 13 granular media access and Android 14 partial-access support.
- Local MediaStore Images/Video collections, DCIM/Camera only, capture-date-first
  ordering and added-date fallback. No per-item file reads during catalog setup.
- Authenticated, pinned native media source with 100-entry catalog pages, strict
  same-origin catalog/thumbnail validation and bounded response reads.
- Bounded thumbnail creation and 8 MiB RAM cache; originals remain separate,
  requested with original media format and served with byte-range support.
- TV lazy grid, date headings, date browsing, loaded-day/item selection, filters,
  selected collection and slideshows. Camera photos use full-size-file TV decode.
- Original video tracks retained without conversion; no 2,000-item timeline cap.

Validation in progress: Kotlin/Compose compile, unit regressions, Android builds/
lint and Android emulator MediaStore/native HTTP instrumentation. Hardware remote
focus, Android permission dialogs on a Samsung phone, 4K/HDR playback and large
real camera libraries require physical device verification.
